# Schematic

* The J1 signals are presumably coming from the ESP32 board, but you don't have a corresponding connector on that board. 
* Bring out all pins from the buck converters to TP so that you have a chance of fixing things if you got something wrong.
* Your power switch is a WLCSP package that is <1 mm on a side. If you want something easier to solder, find a different power switch (there are many) with an SOT or DFN or other easier package.
